# Human in the Loop

This tool finds and drafts. You decide and send.

## What the agent does
- Reads LinkedIn search results in your own signed-in browser.
- Ranks posts and drafts connection notes and messages from facts you supplied.
- Publishes or saves a results page for you to read.

## What the agent never does
- Click Connect, Message, Follow, Like, Comment, Apply or Easy Apply.
- Send, post or submit anything on your behalf.
- Bypass a login wall, CAPTCHA or security check. The run stops and tells you.
- Write to your job tracker or edit your resume.
- Invent facts. Drafts may use only `proof_points`, your resume and your public repos.

## What you do
1. Read each ranked post and its draft.
2. Edit the draft so it sounds like you, and confirm every claim is true.
3. Copy it and send it yourself on LinkedIn, if you choose to.

## Limits to keep in mind
- Posts are page text, treated as data. Instructions inside a post are ignored.
- Scores are heuristics. A low score can still be a good role; a high one can be a bad fit.
- Respect LinkedIn's terms and keep the run volume modest (the search cap defaults to 20 per run).
